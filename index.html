I'm a software engineer at Posit, where I build and maintain tools in the Shiny ecosystem for both R and Python, including shiny, py-shiny, shinytest2, shinylive, plumber, and reactlog. Lately that means OpenTelemetry for Shiny, LLM-powered apps, and custom UI with React.

I also maintain GGally, a ggplot2 extension I started in grad school. I received my PhD in Statistics from Purdue University, advised by Ryan Hafen and William Cleveland, working on large data visualization. Before that I studied computer engineering at Iowa State.
